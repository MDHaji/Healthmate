Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026
App Name: HealthMate

Welcome to HealthMate. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application to book doctor appointments.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we may collect:

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address, and age.
  • Health Information: Appointment history, symptoms, or medical notes you share for booking purposes.
  • Device Data: Basic technical information like device type and IP address for app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Book and manage your appointments with healthcare providers.
  • Send you appointment confirmations, reminders, and updates.
  • Improve app functionality and customer support.

3. Data Sharing & Security

  • With Doctors: Your booking details are shared only with the specific doctor or clinic you choose to visit.
  • Third Parties: We never sell, trade, or rent your personal or medical data to advertisers.
  • Security: We use standard encryption and security measures to keep your data safe.

4. Your Choices

You can update your profile information or delete your account at any time directly through the app settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@healthmateapp.com.
